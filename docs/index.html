Leaf disease severity

Segments the leaf with the excess-green index and Otsu's threshold, finds necrotic lesions by their CIELAB colour, and reports severity as lesion area / leaf area. This is a JavaScript port of the Python pipeline in plant-cell-image-analysis; your photo never leaves the browser.

Works best with one or a few leaves on a plain, non-green, non-brown background. Large photos are scaled to 600 px.
